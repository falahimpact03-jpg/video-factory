Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Video Factory ("the app") is a private desktop tool operated by its owner for uploading videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data accessed

The app requests only the youtube.upload scope. It uses it to upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails created by the owner. It does not read, collect or share any personal data from YouTube or Google accounts.

Data storage

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent to third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
